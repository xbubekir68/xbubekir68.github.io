## Direction contract

THESIS: xbubekir as a calm working chat tool in the category canon, played straight: neutral dark shell, answers as reading text, the composer card as the one prominent object. It refuses the old neon arcs, glows, gradient buttons, glass and the oversized centred logo hero.

OWN-WORLD: neutral greys with a faint violet undertone: sidebar darkest, main one step lighter, composer and menus one step lighter again; 1px hairlines at 8–12% white. Violet appears only on the send button, focus ring, current selection marks, links and the brand marks. System UI sans, 14–15px chrome, 16–17px reading text, tabular numerals in tables, themed selection, caret and scrollbars. Depth only on the composer and dialogs, soft and offset.

STORY: The person opens the app, is greeted by name under a small xbu, types, reads the answer as plain text with quiet copy/again actions; the brain and image attach live inside the composer.
