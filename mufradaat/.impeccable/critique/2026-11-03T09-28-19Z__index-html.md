## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment.** Mufradaat is two products wearing one skin.

**The road is clearly authored for this app.**
- Every Etappe has a vocalized Arabic header and its own road surface.
- Stop names are written for German speakers ("Zähnchen mit Punkten", "Haken und Zähne").
- Tasks hinge on dots and vowel marks.
- The final sign is the Basmala in Qur'anic script.
- No other course could reuse any of it.

**The shell around the road is the old vocabulary trainer.**
- Home is an XP ring with a 7-day chart and "0 von 330 gelernt".
- Spiele is three plain rows.
- 34 of 35 badges are about words.
- Statistiken only counts words.
- Home shows no Arabic on first launch. The "Qur'an-ready" promise lives on one tab.

**Deterministic scan.** The scan found 38 issues in `index.html`: 36 advisory mismatches with DESIGN.md and 2 slop warnings.

Where the review and the scan agree:
- Path-stop words are 17px (line 150), below DESIGN.md's 22px minimum.
- Inline Arabic in tip cards is 21px with line-height 1 (line 264), which breaks the harakat rule.

What only the scan caught: **Arabic type has no scale.**
- Of the 22 off-scale font sizes, about 17 are Arabic. They run from 17 to 110px, with nearly every step in between (34, 36, 38, 40, 42, 44, 46, 48, 56, 60…).
- DESIGN.md documents four Arabic sizes.
- Eleven colors aren't tokens: the "begonnen" chip in both themes, the progress pips (#c99a2e), the level-up sparks (#e8b64c), and the middle stops of the start-screen gradient.
- Two corner radii are off-scale (18px, 5px).

False positives:
- Both slop warnings, "marquee" and "dark-glow", are the splash screen's loading bar. It's intentional, hidden from screen readers, and switches off under reduced motion.
- The #000 at line 44 is a mask stop that never shows as a colour.
- The 22px and 34px sizes are icons.
- The 2px radius is confetti.

**Visual overlays.** None are available. The app's own security policy blocked the scanning script on all three views tested (Start, Lernpfad, Karten), both as a script tag and when fetched.

## Overall Impression

The new reading path is the best part of the app and the clearest expression of what it is. It's the hidden option, though: a new learner lands on the old path, gets quizzed on letters nobody has shown them, and is greeted by statistics that say they've done nothing. **The single biggest opportunity is to make the road the app:** one path, and a Home that is simply the next stop.

## What's Working

1. **The Winding Road makes the promise visible.** Each Etappe has a vocalized name, a landscape and a goal sentence, ending at the Basmala. The Qur'an goal is shown as a destination, not claimed. Stop labels for screen readers are good, e.g. "Lektion 2: Jīm bis Dhāl, gesperrt".
2. **The tasks are designed around the script.** They drill dot positions and contrasts that hinge on vowel marks, and Arabic is shown large: 110px on intro cards and about 30px in options. No vowel marks were clipped in task options. Mistakes come back at the end instead of costing hearts.
3. **Skipping ahead is well built where it exists.** The adaptive Einstufungstest, "Hierher springen" on every Etappe, and a word search that ignores vowel marks (كتاب finds كِتَاب).

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Two curricula are live, and new learners get the wrong one.**
- **What:** Home's "Weiterlernen" depends on a hidden flag (`S.np`, line 1724). Fresh profiles go to the old path. Its first screen asks "Wie heißt dieser Buchstabe?" about ت, offering four names nobody has taught yet. The new path, with Surenanfänge and Koranschrift, sits behind "Vorschau öffnen". On top of that sit two letter groupings, two mastery rules, two skip tests, two help texts, and a preview note written like a roadmap ("Der neue Lernpfad ersetzt den bisherigen, sobald …").
- **Why it matters:** Your principle "reading is the first win" turns into "failure is the first experience". Every inconsistency in the table above traces back to this.
- **Fix:** Make the new path the default for every profile that hasn't started the old one. Move the old path behind a "Bisheriger Lernpfad" link. Delete the old "Schrift · Die Buchstaben" section. Keep one placement test, one mastery rule and one help text.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`

**[P1] Progress is easy to lose: interruptions, the back gesture, and reset.**
- **What:**
  - The X exits a lesson instantly, and reopening restarts at the tip card. This was confirmed live after 6 tasks.
  - The Android back gesture closes the app from anywhere, mid-lesson included.
  - "Fortschritt zurücksetzen" looks like an ordinary grey row. Two taps at any interval wipe XP, streak, badges, letters and lessons, with no list of what's lost and no export for guests.
  - Lesson 1 has 18 tasks, not the promised 12–15, so there's more to lose.
- **Why it matters:** Your learners use short, interrupted phone sessions. Losing 6 tasks to a stray swipe is the moment people stop coming back.
- **Fix:**
  - Save the lesson's task list and position, and show "Weitermachen · Aufgabe 7 von 18" on the stop and on Home.
  - Push one history entry per screen and map the back gesture to each screen's own back target.
  - Make reset a dialog that lists what will go, with a brick-red confirm button, and offer an export for guests.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Keyboard and screen-reader focus is lost after every tap.**
- **What:** Every interaction replaces the whole screen (`paint`, line 1657), and focus falls back to the page itself. Selected pair tiles and the Favorit star have no pressed state. Matched pairs fade to 35%. Wort-Blitz has a fixed 60-second timer with no untimed option.
- **Why it matters:** A screen-reader user has to re-find the question after every answer, in every lesson.
- **Fix:** After each repaint, move focus to the task prompt when the task changes, or back to the same control when it doesn't. Add `aria-pressed` to selected and toggled controls. Offer Wort-Blitz without a timer.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Home and progress count words, but the course now starts with reading.**
- **What:**
  - There is no welcome and no question about the learner's goal or level.
  - Home greets a first-time guest with "0 / 100 XP" and "Lerne heute, um deine Serie zu halten" (with no streak yet), and the main action is cut off by the tab bar.
  - Letter lessons don't move the 7-day chart or Statistiken.
  - Only one badge relates to reading.
  - Karten and the games serve greetings to someone still learning letters.
- **Why it matters:** Amina, who wants to read the Qur'an, never sees her goal on Home, and her real progress shows up as zero.
- **Fix:**
  - Make Home the next stop on the road: the next letter large, the Etappe's goal sentence, and one button. Move stats one tap away.
  - In Stufe 1, count lessons, Etappen and letters marked sicher, and add one badge per Etappe.
  - Unlock Karten and the games stage by stage.
  - Ask about goal and level once, on first launch.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable onboard`

**[P2] Arabic type has no scale.**
- **What:** About 17 different Arabic sizes are in use. Path-stop words are 17px and tip-card Arabic is 21px at line-height 1, both below DESIGN.md's rules (22px minimum, line height 1.4 or more).
- **Why it matters:** The script is the product. When every screen picks its own size, the hierarchy between letter, word and sentence is never learned. Small Arabic in cramped lines is the first thing to break when someone zooms in.
- **Fix:** Define a 6-step Arabic scale, for example 20 / 24 / 30 / 44 / 64 / 96. Map every Arabic class onto it, and record it in DESIGN.md. Raise tip-card Arabic to at least 24px at line height 1.5.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable typeset`

- "Welcher Buchstabe klingt so?" shows a written description of the sound, not audio.
- Karten, Spiele and Wortliste are full of words she can't read yet.

**Markus (reads the script, wants to skip ahead):**
- Two skip systems on two paths.
- The audio-first placement test doesn't say how many questions it has.
- After placing out of reading, the new path ends at "entsteht gerade". To get vocabulary he has to switch to the old path, where another 10-question letter test stands in his way.

## Minor Observations

- **Navigation:**
  - Wortliste opened from Mehr goes back to Start, while Statistiken opened from Home goes back to Mehr.
  - Alphabet is missing from Mehr.
  - Mehr rows have empty sub-lines.
  - Abzeichen and Statistiken are listed on both Home and Mehr.
- **Outdated copy:**
  - The Hörübung row says it needs a device voice, but it plays bundled audio.
  - Its prompt says "Wort" while the options are phrases.
  - The privacy page still says pronunciation comes only from the device voice.
- **Labels:**
  - The old path's sentence button has a contradictory aria-label: "noch gesperrt, 2 von 10 Sätzen frei".
  - "Tagesziel" is filed under "Spiel" in settings.
- **Settings:** the reminder is ON by default but only fires while the page is open.
- **Favoriten:** words can only be starred in Karten, and favourites aren't used anywhere else.
- **Motion:** every tab switch fades in for about 0.5 s, long enough to catch screens half-drawn.
- **Network:** every launch requests `vendor/supabase.js`, which returns 404. The folder doesn't exist in the repo either.
- **Colors and radii:** the eleven untokenised colors and two off-scale radii need to be either tokens or deleted.

## Questions to Consider

- If stage 1 of the new path is complete, what would break if new learners never saw the old path at all?
- Who is Home for? What if it were just the next stop on the road?
- Should Spiele and Karten unlock with the road, Etappe by Etappe, instead of being there from day one?
- For a learner whose goal is the Qur'an, is XP the right measure, or is it "Wörter der Fātiḥa, die du schon lesen kannst"?
